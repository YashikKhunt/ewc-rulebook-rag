Game-title rule governs

VALORANT Article 5.5.1 and EWC Global Rulebook 2026 Article 5.1.9 state different rules on map selection. Under the precedence rule the game-title book governs.

The higher-seeded team elects which side of the veto sequence to open, and the losing side of the coin toss removes the first map (VALORANT — Article 5.5.1, p.11). The veto must be completed no later than fifteen minutes before the scheduled start time (VALORANT — Article 5.5.1.1, p.11).

The Global Rulebook sets the tournament-wide default that map selection procedure is fixed by the applicable game-title rulebook (EWC Global Rulebook 2026 — Article 5.1.9, p.24); the VALORANT procedure governs here.
